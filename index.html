About

I work at the intersection of artificial intelligence, technology strategy, and organizational communication, building systems, analyzing outcomes, and translating complexity into clarity.

Pursuing an MSc in IT, Communication & Organization at Aarhus BSS, I bring together formal academic training in machine learning and data analysis with applied experience designing and deploying AI-powered tools. My focus lies in understanding how AI systems integrate into organizations, shape communication, and inform decision-making at scale.

A BA in International Business Communication from Aarhus University established the analytical and strategic foundations I build on today. An exchange semester at the University of Vermont sharpened my perspective on how technology operates across different organizational and cultural contexts.

My approach is pragmatic and evidence-driven. I work with AI not as a novelty, but as a consequential tool that demands rigor, critical thinking, and precise communication.
